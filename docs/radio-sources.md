- Content statement: The station describes itself as Creative Commons radio; the [Creative Commons directory entry](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/The_Radio_CC) says it streams free music released under Creative Commons and other free licenses.
- Verification on 2026-11-03: HTTPS endpoint returned HTTP 200 with `audio/mpeg`; a local Chrome E2E test verifies playback state and advancing media time through the app's existing audio element.

The stream is played directly from the station and is not downloaded, cached, proxied, or re-broadcast by AstraDJ. Programming and endpoint availability are controlled by the station and may change.
